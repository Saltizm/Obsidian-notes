# PDPA

**Note the difference between these 3 words

Privacy - a state in which one is not observed or disturbed by other people. / the state of being free from public attention.

Security - the state of being free from danger or threat. / procedures followed or measures taken to ensure the security of a state or organization.

Protection - a document guaranteeing immunity from harm to the person specified in it. / a person or thing that protects someone or something.
