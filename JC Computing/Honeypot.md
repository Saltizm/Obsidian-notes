# Honeypot

Honeypot are series of servers (Honeynets) that appear to legitamate servers with intentional vulnerabilities.

Purpose:
- to bait attackers away from the main servers (by making hackers think they got what the need )
- to detect intrusions
- collect infomation on attack to monitor and study
