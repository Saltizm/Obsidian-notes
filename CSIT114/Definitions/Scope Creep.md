# Scope Creep

[[CSIT224/Scope]] creep is **when a project's scope changes after it has started**. It's generally considered harmful and can delay a project and cause team morale to drop.

A good way to prevent Scope creep is to utilise a [[Scope statement]] to define the scope of a project. 
Making it easier to stay within scope.
