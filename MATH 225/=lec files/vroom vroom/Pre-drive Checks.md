# Pre-drive Checks

## Exterior
light Check:
- Exterior light
- headlight
- brake light
- indicator
- hazard light
- reverse light
Windscreen and window:
- no cracks
- clean
- good working condition
Tyres:
- punctures
- [[wear]] (thread depth)
- air pressure
Vehicle body:
- chassie
	- external damage
	- door closed
	- boot closed
	- bonnet closed
- under
	- obstacles
	- fluid leaks
