# Nessus scanner

**Nessus** is a **vulnerability scanner** developed by Tenable, widely used by security professionals to identify vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and compliance issues in networks, servers, and applications.

---

## **What is Nessus?**

- A **commercial vulnerability assessment tool** (with a free limited version: Nessus Essentials).
    
- Detects security flaws such as:
    - Missing patches (OS, software)
    - Misconfigurations (firewalls, services)
    - Default/weak credentials (passwords etc)
    - Compliance violations (PCI-DSS, HIPAA, CIS benchmarks)
    - Abnormal network traffic
- Used for **proactive security auditing** rather than exploitation.
