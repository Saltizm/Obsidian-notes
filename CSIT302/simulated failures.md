# simulated failures

- A **partial test** (or **simulated failure test**) is a controlled validation of a system’s resilience
- done through intentionally disrupting **specific components** 
- assess recovery processes. 

Unlike a [[full-Interruption test]] it does not completely halt operations but instead mimics failures in isolated areas to minimize business impact while still verifying disaster readiness.
