# Assault(CTM)

- Attacker aim to **damage data and software** 
- to stump or alter function of the system 

### Purpose 
#### Obfuscation
- to cover their tracks 
- techniques used:
	- confusion
	- deterrence 
	- diversion

### Technique 

#### Diversion
##### Lateral movement

attackers entering through lateral movement from a more vulnerable server to avoid detection from target.

##### Public WiFi
public wifi is a point of [[Security Vulnerability]] to access untrained individual that is part of the target.
Difficult to trace as many use the network throughout the day.
