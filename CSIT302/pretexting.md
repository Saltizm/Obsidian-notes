- Attacker produce a pretext (made up scenario)
- Used to convince victim to gain their trust/authority

### Traits 
#### Role playing 
- User assumes a false identity 
- This can be:
	- A guy that has just been in a car crash urging their friend(victim) to pay them for the hospital bill
	- Ugandan prince 
#### Planned narrative
- Craft a believable story to justify their request
	- A siren playing in the background/ coughing while speaking
	- My government is trying to take my gold
