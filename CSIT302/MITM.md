- Man in the middle attack
- Intercepting packets in a network through [[IP spoofing]]
### Examples
- [[ARP poisoning]]
- [[SSL strip]]
- [[DNS poisoning]]
### Uses 
- Sniffing sensitive Information:
	- For:
	- [[Lateral movement]]
	- [[Vertical privilege escalation]]
- Checking what websites are being visited
	- For:
	- [[Internal reconnaissance]]
