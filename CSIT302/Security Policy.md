# Security Policy

- Set of ***criteria*** for provision of security service ^1ccf53
- The ***statement*** of required protection for the information object
- A set of ***rules*** that govern all aspects of security related system and system element behavior 
refers to the [[Security Objective|objective]] and [[Security constraints|constraints]] 

- A security policy is a living document
	- revised and updated to meet current [[Threat Landscape]]

Policy is law $\rightarrow$ procedures and standards are instructions $\rightarrow$ [[Best practices|Guidelines]] are suggestions 
					$\rightarrow$ Increasingly technical $\rightarrow$
					$\rightarrow$ Decreasingly Enforced $\rightarrow$
### Anatomy 
#### Pre planning checklist
- Well defined scope
- Current industry practices 
### Policy
- Policy is law 
- Must be easily understood to be followed 
- Need to be enforced 
### Procedure/Standard
- procedure is instructions
- Standard is the status quo
- All requirements in policy must be met in Standard
- Minimally technical, enough to communicate needs 
### Guidelines (best practices)
- Guidelines are suggestions 
- Highly technical, used to inform technical personal who are not in cybersec of:
	- [[Security Vulnerability|Security risks]] during maintenance or development
	- Practical examples (best practices) $\rightarrow$ *role specific*
