People tend to follow the usual flow of events. this can be exploited for [[Phishing]]

### Example
- Foot in the door
	- Start with a harmless request 
		- Ask if their email is active 
	- Escalate 
		- Send link for them to update their password saying it has been compromised 

- practical examples:
- Scammer calls pretending to be from "Microsoft Support" and asks, _"Have you noticed slow performance on your PC?"_ (victim says yes).
