The exchange between two parties for short term gains. 
Social engineering:
- attacks offer a benefit in exchange for [[Compromising]] information.
- Low success rate

Social engineering principle:
- [[Reciprocation]]
### Example
1. **The Offer** – The attacker poses as a helpful entity (IT support, researcher, vendor, etc.) and offers something valuable (free tech support, gifts, exclusive access).
        
2. **The Request** – In return, they ask for login credentials, payment, remote access, or other sensitive actions.
    
3. **The Exploitation** – Once the victim complies, the attacker steals data, installs malware, or gains unauthorized access.
