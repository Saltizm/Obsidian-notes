# Phishing

performing malicious acts through **Impersonation** of **Legitimate entities**.
Goals
- receiving money
- receiving sensitive information
- installing malware for [[Compromising]] systems

Key principle used:
- [[Liking]]
- [[Reciprocation]]
### Type

#### By delivery
| Social media phishing                                           | Email phishing/Smishing (sms phishing)                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Using social media post, DMs or profiles to send phishing links | using fake email/sms of trusted sources to send phishing links/files |

#### By target
| Spear phishing                         | Whaling                                  | BEC(business email compromise)                                                   |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Highly targeted at specific individual | Targeting high ranking individual(whale) | Impersonating company executive using business email to wire money from employee |
#### By Technique
| Deceptive phishing                   | Pharming                                                                | [[MITM phishing]]                                            | Evil twin Phishing                  | Angler phishing                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| phish using deceptive files or links | Phishing by redirecting from legitimate sites through [[DNS poisoning]] | Faking network requests by interception connections to phish | Posing as fake copy of Wifi network | Posing as customer support(angler) to phish |
