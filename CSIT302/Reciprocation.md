# Reciprocation

people tend to feel obligated to return a favor. This can be exploited for [[social engineering]]. 

> [!example]- Example
> - Tailgating
> 	- holding a door for someone in target building to follow them in
> - Bait and switch
> 	- providing freeware before asking for payment later
