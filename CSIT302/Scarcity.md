# Scarcity

Threaten victims with short supply (in product or time) of something they need.

> [!example]- Example
> - Time sensitive sales
> 	- Give users deals that are expiring on [[Phishing]] sites to bait them into giving up their personal details
> - Panic buying
> 	- Users tend to be more susceptible to scams when product are selling out fast
