- Information regarding potential or ongoing threats 
### Types of intelligence
#### Tactical threat intelligence
- Reports on [[CVE(Malware and exploits)|methods]] used by threat actors
- More technical 
##### Use case:
- MNC/SME - To update [[IDS and IPS]] rules 
- Individuals - Update software and device systems
--- 
#### Operational threat intelligence 
- Report on potential operations against an organisation 
- somewhat technical
- Consist of:
	- Intel on current affairs amongst threat actors in forums (human readable)
	- documents on leaked data (machine readable)
#### Threat profiling
![[Threat profiling]]
##### Use case
- MNC/SME - To prepare for potential attacks in the near future
---
#### Strategic threat intelligence
- Reports on recent [[Threat Landscape]] trends
- Less technical
##### Use case
- MNC - For future business strategic planning 
---
### Why use threat intelligence
- Deciphering large number of security alerts
- [[Incidence response]] -- to help find the root cause
- [[Alert triage]] -- to determine the most important threat during an attack
