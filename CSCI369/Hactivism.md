# Hactivism

- Using online media to **mobilise** people to conventional lobbying (writing to legislators, signature for cause etc.)
- Wide scope of motivation
- Ranges from **Specific causes** or **Political opinions**

Hactivism (Hate campaigns) are usually covertly funded by 
- Governments
- Single issue campaign groups
### Case study
Wael Ghonim - Used social media to trigger the Arab Spring 
Time frame - 2011

#### Consequences 
DDOS - Companies and individuals cannot operate as they received a slew of angry emails and tweets.
Brand Damage - Doxing campaigns and Slander brought out private information that defaced individuals or companies.
