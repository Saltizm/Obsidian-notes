# Perception

How information is acquired from the world and transformed into [[Information storage (memory)|long term memory]] (experiences).

How perceivable a piece of information is comes down to how easily it is processed by the [[InformationIO|senses]]

eg.
icons should be easily understood ([[Heuristics|design pattern/Heuristics]])
bordering and spacing for grouping
sound should be audible and distinguishable 
important elements should be distinguishable
