Attenuation is the loss of signal strength.  
denoted in decibels (dB)
Since it scales in decibels the loss of signal strength is exponential.

formula for decibel.
  dB = 10 x log10 (P2 / P1)
  whereby, 
  P1 is starting signal power level (V)
  P2 is ending signal power level (V) 

The 10 factors of attenuation:
- Distance
- [[Medium]]
- [[Frequency]]
- [[ Interference]]
- Absorption
- Reflection and Refraction
- Connectors and Joints
- [[ Scattering]]
- Impurities and defects
- Temperature and Environmental Factors

Attenuation is additive.
This means that the sign of the change in attenuation has to be shown**
