# Fibre

[[wired|Fibre optics]] cables consist of a thin glass cable about slightly thicker than a human hair surrounded by a plastic buffer.

A photo diode/laser generate pulses of light to be transmitted through the cable and received at a photo receptor.

| pro                                             | con                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Extremely high bandwidth                        | Susceptible to reflection/refraction       |
| Elastic traffic carrying capacity               | Costly (dropping)                          |
| Not affected by [[induction]]                   | Special test equipment (difficult to test) |
| low error rate                                  | Frail                                      |
| tough to temper with (secure detection) | Susceptible to environmental factors                                            |
| low in weight                                   |                                            |
