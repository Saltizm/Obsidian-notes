[[wired|Twisted-pair]] cables have a core consisting of 2 wires that are twisted.

| pros                     | cons                                    |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| High availibility        | limited [[spectrum]]                    |
| low cost of installation | limited [[bits\|data rates]]            |
| Ease of replacement      | High susceptibility to [[Interference]] |
|                          | High error rate                                        |

The twisting brings the wires **near perpendicular** to reduce [[Crosstalk]] interference. 

![[crosstalk.PNG]]

twisted-pair cables have metal shielding to minimise [[Crosstalk]] between the wires.
![[metalShielding.PNG|500]]
However, more expensive twisted-pair cables have an extra metal shielding to reduce [[Crosstalk]] from neighbouring wires.
