# quantization level

Quantization level is the size of the [[Sampling rate|sample]] snapshot taken.

The larger the [[bandwidth]] of a signal the higher the quantization level of the snapshot.

The quantization level in determined in powers of 2(  i.e 128(7bits), 256(8bits) ). 
This is because snapshots are taken in [[Bits]].
