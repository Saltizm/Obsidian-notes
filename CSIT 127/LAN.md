- Local Area Networks( LAN ) are networks that [[Devices|individual computer]] systems are connected with. 
- It spans from the size of a *household* to a *company*( 100 - 1000m )

LAN are connected either using cables(LAN) or Wireless connections(WLAN).

The devices are connected in terms of a [[Network Topology]] which would determine the characteristic of the network.

**LAN typically have medium to high speed connections.**

---

Fundamentally, a network are [[computers]] connected through the use of various [[components]]. 

a [[computers]] consist of a [[NIC]] which it uses to connect to a hub-like device like a [[Network Switch]] or another [[computers|computer]].
