A signal is a [[electromagnetic]] or electric current that carries [[Data]]. It is represented in a wave function in a time voltage graph.

A signal has 3 components:
[[Amplitude]]
[[Frequency]]
[[Phase]]

A [[Frequency|signal frequency]] determines the [[spectrum]] and [[bandwidth]] of the signal.

Signals are separated into 2 types. [[Analog]] and [[Digital]].
( i.e the human voice has a spectrum of 300-3100hz, a bandwidth of 2800hz )

| characteristic | analog      | digital   |
| -------------- | ----------- | --------- |
| accuracy       | more        | less      |
| decoding       | difficult   | easy      |
| process        | easy        | difficult |
| security       | unencrypted | encrypted |
| bandwidth      | low         | high      |
