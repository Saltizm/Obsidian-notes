# Crosstalk

Crosstalk is unwanted signals in a communication channel(ie telephone, radio) caused by transference of energy from another wire.

There are two ways this can happen:
- leakage
	- a wire is ungrounded and loose current get into the communication channel
- coupling
	- a wire produce a small current in the communication through [[induction]].
