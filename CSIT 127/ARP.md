# ARP

Address resolution protocol( ARP ) is used for mapping the MAC address to an [[IP|IP address]]. 
It is found in layer 2 of the [[OSI model]] , datalink.

## Function
- To translate [[IP|IP addresses]]  into [[NIC|Mac address]]
