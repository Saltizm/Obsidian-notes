# PAN

Personal Area Network( PAN ) is the smallest type of network. It is no larger than *10 meters*.

PAN consist of mostly of wireless connections through *infrared* or *Bluetooth* technology.

PAN are usually [[ad havoc]]

(i.e connecting a Bluetooth speaker to a television and using a remote to change channels)
