Wide Area Network( WAN ) consist of many [[LAN]] interconnected spanning multi-continentally( 1000+km ).

WAN are connected through data centers using heavy and expensive *Submarine cables*.

(i.e A computer in Singapore can be connected to Australia through the *[[internet]]*)

A [[LAN]] is interconnected in a WAN(internet) through a [[Modem|dial-up modem]](defunct), [[Modem|cable modem]] or DSL.

![[ComputerToWAN.png]]

However in cases where a [[LAN]] is connected to another [[LAN]] in a company it can be done using either a [[Network Switch]] or a [[bus]]. 

![[LANTOLAN.PNG]]
Then the networks are connected to their local ISP( internet service provided ).
The local ISP connects to a Regional ISP then an International ISP.
Finally, the International ISP are connected by [[Network access point|NAP( network access point )]].
