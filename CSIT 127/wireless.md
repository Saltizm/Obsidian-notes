# wireless

Wireless data transmission rely on the the [[electromagnetic]] spectrum.

| media type | bandwidth     | performance (MBps)  | repeater spacing |
| ---------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| [[satellite]]  | 100GHz        | $10^{-7}$-$10^{-9}$ | long-very long   |
| [[microwave]]  | 100GHz        | $10^{-7}$-$10^{-9}$ | medium           |
| [[infrared]]   | 300GHz-430THz | 1.15                | short                 |

The higher the frequency the lower the range as disruption from [[noise]] becomes more significant.
