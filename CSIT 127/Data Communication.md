# Data Communication

Transfer of [[Data]], *digital* or *analog* using a digital or analog [[Signal]].

![[Datacommdiagram.PNG]]


The [[Signal|signal(message)]] can be transferred through different types of [[medium]]. 
[[Signal]] can be affected by [[Signal Distortion|noise]].

[[Protocol|protocols]] are rules that determines how devices communicate.

When there are many connections. A [[network]] forms.
