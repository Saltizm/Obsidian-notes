# Frequency division multiplexing( FDM )

The bandwidth is split into sections( bands ) to transfer [[Signal]] from multiple devices continuously.

![[FDM.PNG]]
each band is separated by guard band. 
guard band are a narrow frequency range used to separate bands.

After the signals has been received it is passed through a low pass filter to remove any additional frequencies or noise.

used in:
- broadcast radio
- television
