# Delta modulation

Delta modulation is method to convert [[Analog]] signals into [[Digital]] signals whereby fidelity is of no importance. ( i.e voice, alarm ).

As the name implies. Delta modulation measure the change in the [[Analog]] signal.

An analog waveform is tracked, using a binary 1 to represent a rise in voltage, and a 0 to represent a drop

The sign of the slope of the [[Analog]] signal at a **snapshot** determines the voltage of the [[Pulse width modulation|PWM wave]].

 However, this can lead to [[Quantizing noise ]] and [[Overloading noise]].
