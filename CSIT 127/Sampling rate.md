# Sampling rate

sampling rate is the number of samples taken in a second in a continuous [[Analog]] signal.

The higher the sampling rate, the more representative the [[digital]] representation of the [[Analog]] signal is.

To get a good representation of the [[Analog]] signal. The sampling rate should follow the [[Nyquist theorem]].
