# Risk mitigation plan

proactive response to threat 
- focuses on threat prevention.
- summary report of potential risk found through [[Threat intelligence]] and [[Quantitative risk analysis]] 
- **Layout**
	- Context and asset identification ( crown jewels )
	- risk identification 
	- risk analysis 
	- risk response 
	- monitor report and review - development of risk register (living documentation to record risks and responses)
