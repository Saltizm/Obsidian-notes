<h5> Fixed price/ Lump sum contracts </h5>
Fixed price for a *well defined* product/service.
Low [[risk]] assumed as potential additional cost ( [[risk]] ) is held responsible by the contractor.

**Use case** :
Fixed cost products : Steel beams, parts
Low risk services : Insurance 

<h5> Cost-reimbursable contracts </h5>
>[!INFO]- ( fuck is the lecture yapping on about )

Fixed price in conjunction to potential future additional fees and incentive fees. 
High [[risk]] assumed as all risk is held by the customer in the form of **additional fees**. 

**Use case** :
Long term projects : Parts designing, land contractors projects, research

<h5> Time and material contracts </h5>
Similar to **Cost-reimbursable contracts**. However, the additional labour cost is paid **Hourly**.
Medium [[risk]] assumed as **Hourly rates** can be negotiated. However there is still potential for unexpected material costs.

Use case :
Short term projects : cybersecurity testing, small scale construction, financial consultation
