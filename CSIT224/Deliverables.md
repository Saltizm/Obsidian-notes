# Deliverables

A deliverable is anything that brings [[Stakeholders|investors and top management]] **information** regarding the project is a deliverable

List of deliverables Documents:
1. [[Project Charter]]
2. [[scope statement]]
3. [[WBS (work breakdown structure)]]
4. [[Risk register]] 
5. [[cost baseline]]
6. schedule
7. status reports
8. final project presentation/report
9. lessons-learned report
10. business case
11. team contract


Any Product-related [[components|components]] are also considered deliverables.
