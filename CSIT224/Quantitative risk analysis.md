# Quantitative risk analysis

Quantitative risk analysis is used to analyse the Risk's **Probability** and **Impact**. 

#### Technique
##### Decision tree analysis
![[Pasted image 20240601185438.png]]

Used to calculate the economic impact of a [[Risk]].

EMV of outcome = Risk Probability x Risk event monetary value
Risk [[event]] - all possible outcome of an [[event]].
>[!INFO]- Example
>Investing in Ai technology to increase efficiency
>Outcome 1: It succeeds ( efficiency increase ) 
>Net gain = 10 000
>
>Outcome 2: It fails ( cost incurred )
>Net gain = -80 000
