<h3> Charisma </h3>
With charisma others perception of you will be better leading to the [[Halo effect]].
The ability to charm and sway others will help push the [[Organisation culture]] in your favor, 
allowing future promotions and stability to improve.

<h3> Power </h3>
Power is the potential ability to influence behavior to get people to do things they would not otherwise do.

<h5> types of power </h5>
**Coercive**
The ability to employ *Threats and punishments* to command. 

**Legitimate** 
*Formal positions of authority* granting ability to command.

**Expert**
Having *knowledge and experience*. command through guidance.

**Reward**
Ability to command others using the promise of *Reward*.

 **Referent** 
Informal positions of authority from *connections* granting ability to command.
