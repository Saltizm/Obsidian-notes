# Risk register

A risk register is **a document that is used as a risk management tool to identify potential setbacks within a project** (Monitoring risks).

![[Pasted image 20240601184049.png]]
 
##### Key elements

| Element     | Explanation                                                                                       |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Probability | chance of risk occurring                                                                          |
| Impact      | The impact of the risk                                                                            |
| Risk owner  | someone who is responsible for managing the risk  ( I.e using [[Risk\|Risk management measures]]) |
