# requirement management plan

a condition or capability that is necessary to be present in a product, service, or result to satisfy a business need

documents **HOW** a project will be analysed, documented and managed to collect [[requirement]].

Instructs on how to :
- plan, track and report **requirement activities**
- configure **management activities**
- prioritise requirements
- use product metrics
- trace and capture attribute of requirement
