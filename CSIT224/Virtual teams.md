# Virtual teams

Virtual Teams  
- Advantages  
o Lowering costs because many virtual workers do not require office space or support beyond their home offices  
o Providing more expertise and flexibility or increasing competitiveness and responsiveness by having team members from across the globe working any time of day or night  
o Improving the work-life balance for team members by eliminating fixed office hours and the need to travel to work.  
- Disadvantages  
o Isolating team members  
o Increasing the potential for communication problems  
o Reducing the ability for team members to network and transfer information informally  
o Increasing the dependence on technology to accomplish work.
