The collective mentality of an organisation that is built upon the beliefs(soft) and philosophy(hard) of the company.

The main determinant of the Organisation culture is :

<h5>The CEO/Managers of the company. </h5>
This is because:
- Organisation culture is enforced through the rules and regulations of the company. (hard)
- The actions of [[Stakeholders|postions of power]] inspires those who are under them. (soft)
	- (i.e If a CEO hold himself accountable the employees will do so too as they look up to them)

<h5> The collegues </h5>
This is because:
- Influence from peers (soft)
