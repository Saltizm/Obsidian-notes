We use big-O notation to asymptotically bound the growth of a running-time of a function (𝑓(𝑛)) to *lower* or *same* order of growth of another function (𝑔(𝑛)) within a constant factor.

![[Pasted image 20250111175146.png]]

g(n) is upper bound of f(n) --> g(n) is *Big O* of f(n) --> **f(n) ∈ O(g(n))**
